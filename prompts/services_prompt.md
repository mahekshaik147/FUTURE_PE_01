# Services Page Prompt

## Objective
Generate website-ready service page copy for a modern cafe.

---

## Prompt

Act as a professional website copywriter for local businesses.

Generate services page content for "Brew Haven Cafe" located in Bangalore.

Include the following sections:

1. Specialty Coffee
2. Breakfast & Brunch
3. Handcrafted Desserts
4. Work-Friendly Space
5. Group Hangouts & Weekend Vibes

For each section include:
- A short description
- Customer benefits
- Why customers love it

Requirements:
- Keep content website-ready
- Use warm and inviting language
- Avoid generic AI wording
- Make the cafe feel modern and welcoming
- Focus on customer experience and atmosphere

Tone:
Friendly, cozy, modern, premium but natural.

Output format:
- Service Name
- Description
- Benefits
- Why People Love It
