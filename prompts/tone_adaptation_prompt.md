# Tone Adaptation Prompt

## Objective
Demonstrate how AI-generated website copy can adapt to different local business types using tone-based prompt engineering.

---

## Prompt

Act as an AI website copy specialist.

Generate homepage copy examples for different local businesses while adapting the tone, language, and messaging style for each business type.

Business Types:
1. Cafe
2. Salon
3. Clinic
4. Digital Marketing Agency

For each business include:
- Homepage headline
- Short intro
- CTA section

Tone Requirements:
- Cafe → Warm, cozy, inviting
- Salon → Elegant, friendly, confidence-boosting
- Clinic → Professional, trustworthy, calm
- Agency → Modern, confident, results-focused

Requirements:
- Make each tone feel unique
- Avoid repetitive AI phrases
- Keep content realistic and website-ready
- Use emotionally appropriate language for each business type

Output format:
- Business Type
- Headline
- Intro
- CTA
